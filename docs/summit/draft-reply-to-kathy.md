A few notes on the data, so the slides stay accurate:

- Your Dataset B card describes "growth sequence, element ratio, and measured grain area for each
  sample". We have grain measurements for only two wafers, so the workable measurement across the
  whole collection is surface roughness: about 1,000 samples, each with the settings it was grown
  with and one microscope measurement of how rough the film came out. We would like to rewrite that
  card to match, if you are willing.
- The mess in it is real and is exactly the kind your slide 9 describes. There are 35 different
  spellings of the material names, including a few where a substrate name was typed into the
  material field; 228 samples have no growth time recorded and 106 were never measured; roughness
  runs from 0.1 to 92 nanometres; and some scans stopped early. We added nothing.
- One honest caution for the framing: nothing in these records shows that these samples became
  chips, or that a smoother film makes a better device. The accurate line is that 2D materials are
  being researched as candidates for future electronics.
